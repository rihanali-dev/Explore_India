About India

India is a fascinating country known for its rich history, colorful culture, and diverse landscapes. From the majestic snow-capped mountains in the north to the tropical sunny beaches and backwaters in the south, India offers an unforgettable travel experience for tourists around the world.

Top Travel Destinations

1. Agra

State: Uttar Pradesh

Description: Agra is world-famous for the iconic Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage site and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture.

Interesting Fact: The Taj Mahal appears to change its color at different times of the day, looking pinkish in the morning, milky white in the evening, and golden under the moon.

2. Jaipur

State: Rajasthan

Description: Known as the Pink City, Jaipur is renowned for its grand royal palaces, historic forts, and traditional handicrafts.

Interesting Fact: The entire city was painted pink in 1876 by Maharaja Sawai Ram Singh to welcome the Prince of Wales as a gesture of hospitality.

3. Goa

State: Goa

Description: A distinguished coastal destination celebrated for its golden beaches, vibrant hospitality scene, recreational water activities, and rich Portuguese architectural heritage.

Interesting Fact: Goa is the smallest state in India by geographical area, yet it boasts over 100 kilometers of scenic coastline.

4. Manali

State: Himachal Pradesh

Description: Nestled within the Himalayan range, this distinguished mountain destination is renowned for its panoramic alpine vistas, world-class river expeditions, advanced trekking trails, and premier skiing facilities.

Interesting Fact: Manali derives its name from the sage Manu, the revered progenitor of humanity in Hindu mythology, embodying ancient lore in every peak..

5. Kerala

State: Kerala

Description: Popularly known as "God's Own Country", featuring serene backwaters, traditional houseboats, lush tea estates, and Ayurvedic wellness.

Interesting Fact: Cochin International Airport in Kerala is the world's very first airport powered completely by solar energy.

Travel Tips

  1. Always keep hard copies as well as digital backups of all identity documents and travel tickets.
  2. Book transport and hotel stays in advance during peak travel seasons.
  3. Keep some emergency cash handy as digital payments may not work in remote areas.
  4. Respect local customs, traditions, and dress modestly when visiting holy places.
  5. Save local emergency numbers and hotel contact details on your mobile phone.
